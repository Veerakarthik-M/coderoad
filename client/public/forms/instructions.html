2. Required Documents

  • Institution ID Card — Current academic year ID card issued by the institution (photocopy + original for verification)
  • Aadhaar Card — Photocopy of student's Aadhaar card
  • Passport Size Photographs — 2 recent colour photographs (3.5 cm × 4.5 cm)
  • Bonafide Certificate — From the Head of Institution, confirming enrolment in current academic year
  • Caste Certificate — Only if applying under SC/ST/OBC concession category
  • Disability Certificate — Only if applying under Differently Abled category
  • Previous Pass — For renewal applications only

3. How to Apply — Step by Step

  1. Register on ANAVANDI Portal
    Visit the ANAVANDI portal and create your student account using your official email ID and mobile number.
  2. Fill the Online Application
    Complete the multi-step form with your personal details, institution details, and travel route (boarding point to destination).
  3. Upload Required Documents
    Upload a clear scan/photo of your Institution ID Card. The file must be JPG, PNG, or PDF and under 2MB.
  4. Institution Verification
    Your institution's admin will verify your enrollment details online. This typically takes 1–3 working days.
  5. KSRTC Approval
    After institution verification, the KSRTC admin will review and approve the application. This takes 2–5 working days.
  6. Digital Pass Issued
    Once approved, your Digital Concession Pass with a QR code is issued. Download or show on your mobile to the conductor.

5. Rules & Regulations

  • The concession pass is strictly non-transferable. Lending the pass to another person will result in cancellation and legal action.
  • The pass is valid only for travel between the declared boarding point and destination.
  • Students must carry the pass at all times while travelling and produce it on demand by the conductor or KSRTC checking staff.
  • The pass must be surrendered immediately on completion of the academic year or on cessation of student status.
  • Any misuse of the pass will result in cancellation and the student/institution may be blacklisted.
  • The pass is valid for one academic year only. Renewal application must be submitted before the start of each new academic year.
  • Route changes must be reported to KSRTC within 7 days. A new pass will be issued for the revised route.

6. Pass Renewal

  • Renewal applications must be submitted 30 days before expiry of the current pass.
  • Students promoted to next class/year/semester must apply for renewal with updated institution ID.
  • Renewal requires the same documents as a new application.
  • Late renewal may result in a gap in concession benefit — no retrospective concession is granted.
